Rules of Four Corners

The goal is to be the **last player remaining** or the **Seeker** if everyone else is caught.

Game Flow 🏃

  1. One player is randomly selected as the **Seeker**. Everyone else is an **Active Player**.
  2. The Seeker closes their eyes (virtually counts down for **5 seconds**).
  3. **Active Players** must simultaneously select one of the four corners (1, 2, 3, or 4).
  4. After the countdown, the **Seeker** points to one of the four corners.

Elimination & Lives 🔵

  • If an **Active Player** is in the corner the Seeker points to, they are **eliminated**.
  • Active Players are marked with a **🔵** (blue circle) next to their name, indicating they **haven't been eliminated yet** (their "Final Life").
  • Once eliminated, the **🔵** disappears, and they are out of the game.

The Rare Comeback (Conditional Revival) 🎲

If the Seeker points to an empty corner and misses **two rounds in a row**, **one random player** from the eliminated list is instantly revived and rejoins the game!

Winning 🎉

  • **Standard Win:** The last Active Player standing wins.
  • **Showdown:** If only one Active Player remains, it's the **FINAL SHOWDOWN**. If the Seeker catches them, the **Seeker wins**. If the Seeker misses, the **Last Player wins**.
